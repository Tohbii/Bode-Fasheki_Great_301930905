
# Case Study: Fashek Clothing Store Management System

## 1. Organisation Overview
Fashek is a small clothing brand that sells clothing items such as shirts, trousers, jeans, and other fashion products. As the business grows, it needs a structured way to manage products, inventory, customers, orders, and payments.

## 2. Problem Statement
Managing product details, available stock, customer orders, and payment records without a centralised database can lead to inconsistent records, stock errors, and difficulty tracking sales.

## 3. Proposed Database System
The proposed system is a database-backed order-management system for Fashek. It will use a relational database to organise business data, maintain relationships between records, and support reliable queries and transactions.

## 4. Main Data Requirements
- Customers and their contact details
- Products, sizes, colours, and prices
- Inventory and available stock
- Customer orders and order items
- Payments and payment status
- Delivery details and fulfilment status

## 5. Scope
The project will use PostgreSQL for relational database implementation and explore query optimisation, transaction management, NoSQL data modelling, and distributed or cloud database concepts.

Sample records used for testing may be simulated and will not represent actual customer information.
